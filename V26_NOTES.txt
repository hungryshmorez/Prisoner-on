CACHE//ZERO v26 — ROUTINE MACHINE

PRISON WORK
- RØ can take one of five prison work assignments:
  * Kitchen Detail
  * Maintenance
  * Laundry Detail
  * Yard Crew
  * Gym Orderly
- Work call runs 07:45–08:55 prison time and temporarily overrides RØ's normal morning route.
- RØ must spend enough real simulation minutes at the correct work location to complete the shift.
- Completed shifts pay credits, build a work streak, improve work reputation, slightly improve the job-related fighter stat, and can affect guard respect.
- Missed work creates infractions, reputation/attention penalties, and repeated misses can suspend the assignment.
- Lockdowns, medical status, segregation, and serious injury can excuse the work call.
- Work accidents depend on assignment, fatigue, energy, and protective/improvised gear.
- Autopilot reports to work and remains on assignment automatically.

NPC ROUTINES
- Named/dynamic inmates receive deterministic work assignments.
- During work call, their physical schedule changes to the corresponding work location.
- WORLD inmate cards now display the inmate's prison job.

BODY TRANSFORMATION
- Training strength and conditioning now feed a persistent visual body profile.
- RØ's sprite bulk and conditioned appearance change gradually rather than only from immediate stats.
- Posture can become guarded, ready, slumped, confident, or neutral based on trauma, aggression, morale, energy, and personality anchors.
- Fights and serious work accidents can leave persistent pixel scars/burns/bruises.
- The FIGHTER view now displays physical profile/posture and permanent marks.

STABILITY
- v25 save migration retained.
- v26 work/body state validates on load and every tick.
- render-loop crash guard and safe-location handling retained.
